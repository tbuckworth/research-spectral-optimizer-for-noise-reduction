\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

The literature search underlying this section was conducted on 2026-07-31
across academic, lab-blog, and community channels (28 curated references).
No discovered work combines (a) per-sample gradient covariance
eigendecomposition with a random-matrix-theory threshold as the training
update rule with (b) evaluation on low-SNR financial prediction; the novelty
claim rests on absence of evidence in an actively producing niche and is
positioned against the nearest neighbor below rather than claimed as a
vacuum.

\paragraph{Per-sample gradient filtering (2024--2026).}
The closest empirical precedent is Gradient Agreement Filtering
\citep{chaubard2024gradientagreementfiltering}, which filters or reweights
macrobatch updates by cross-microbatch gradient agreement using pairwise
orthogonality/correlation checks, improving robustness under
image-classification label noise. The Spectral Optimizer differs in both
mechanism (full $B \times B$ similarity-matrix eigendecomposition with a
Marchenko--Pastur-style threshold, rather than pairwise checks) and domain
(low-SNR financial regression, which GAF never touched). A burst of adjacent
work confirms per-sample gradient statistics are a live optimizer-design
surface: orthogonal-gradient constraints against noisy-label memorization
\citep{mai2026orthogonalgradient}, per-sample clipping with heavy-tailed
convergence theory \citep{nobile2026persampleclipping}, gradient spectral
entropy for backdoor-sample detection \citep{zhao2026gradsentry}, and
per-sample momentum with low-pass filtering for DP-SGD \citep{xu2025dppmlf}.
None uses covariance-eigenspectrum thresholding as the update rule.

\paragraph{Why consensus filtering might work---and why it might not.}
The theoretical ancestor is the Coherent Gradients hypothesis
\citep{chatterjee2020coherentgradients}: SGD generalizes because gradient
directions shared across many examples are reinforced more than
idiosyncratic ones---an intuition independently articulated in the community
\citep{pope2022general_circuits}, and related to broader treatments of the
memorization--generalization spectrum \citep{vaintrob2025memorization}. The
sharpest counter-hypothesis is \citet{feldman2019longtailmemorization}:
memorization of rare long-tail examples can be \emph{necessary} for
generalization, so a consensus filter could suppress rare genuine signal in
heavy-tailed financial data. The noisy-label literature operates mostly at
the loss/data level \citep{liu2020earlylearningregularization,
liang2022noisylabelsurvey, song2025labelnoisesurvey}; optimizer-level
gradient-covariance filtering is a comparatively unexplored corner of that
space.

\paragraph{Random matrix theory in finance.}
Marchenko--Pastur eigenvalue cleaning of financial \emph{return} correlation
matrices is a mature, $\sim$20-year quantitative-finance literature
\citep{pafka2004rmtexponentialweighting, potters2005rmtoldlaces,
bouchaud2009rmtfinancereview, lakshtanov2023denoisingcorrelation}: large
fractions of empirical eigenspectra are indistinguishable from noise, and
clipping them improves portfolio risk estimates. The Spectral Optimizer
transposes this denoising logic from returns covariance (a post-hoc
estimation step) to per-sample \emph{gradient} covariance during training;
the transposition appears unpublished. Our results delimit it: on this task
the analogy breaks because the gradient similarity spectrum turns out to be
provably target-independent (Section~\ref{sec:exp001}).

\paragraph{The baseline is not naive.}
Gradient noise in deep learning is structured and heavy-tailed
\citep{xie2022overlookedstructure, dang2025heavytailedstability}, and
sign-based/adaptive normalization---what Adam already does---confers
meaningful robustness in exactly this regime \citep{yu2026signheavytailed};
robust adaptive variants date back to at least \citet{gulcehre2017robustadaptive}.
A 2026 benchmark of 15 optimizers on tabular MLPs finds Muon consistently
beats AdamW \citep{gorishniy2026benchmarkingoptimizers}, so ``beats
default-hyperparameter Adam'' would be a strawman claim. We therefore adopt
the codified fair-comparison methodology: matched tuning budgets, multiple
seeds, standardized reporting \citep{schneider2019deepobs,
blauth2024fastoptimizerbenchmark}, with tuned AdamW as the primary opponent
and the filter-on/filter-off ablation at identical base configuration as the
cleanest mechanism isolation. As a contrast at the mechanism level, the
gradient noise scale \citep{mccandlish2018gradientnoisescale} treats
gradient noise as a scalar SNR statistic for batch-size selection, and
K-FAC-style methods model gradient covariance for curvature preconditioning
\citep{martens2018kfacrnn}---neither performs noise-rejection filtering of
the update.

\paragraph{Evaluation protocol for Numerai-style data.}
No academic Numerai literature was found; the practitioner-standard protocol
comes from community sources: era-based purged cross-validation with
embargo, per-era correlation as the metric \citep{numerai_forum2021_eras},
and feature-neutral correlation to separate genuine signal from feature
overfitting \citep{numerai_forum2021_fnc}. MLPs remain standard and
competitive for tabular data \citep{gorishniy2021revisitingtabular}. One
structural constraint shaped our design: Numerai asset identifiers reset
each era, so a longitudinal recurrent arm is impossible on this dataset; the
sequence arm uses a within-era framing (Section~\ref{sec:exp005}).
