\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

\subsection{The Spectral Optimizer}
\label{sec:method-optimizer}

The object under test is fixed in writing (amendment F11): the
\texttt{SpectralConsensusFilter}, the exact $B \times B$ variant from the
prior project's \texttt{spectral\_optimizer.py}, reused byte-identically
(verified), not re-implemented. At each step it (i) computes per-sample
gradients $g_1,\dots,g_B$ via \texttt{torch.func} \texttt{vmap(grad)};
(ii) row-normalizes them and forms the $B \times B$ similarity matrix
$S_{ij} = \hat g_i^\top \hat g_j$; (iii) eigendecomposes $S$ and keeps
eigendirections above a Marchenko--Pastur-style threshold (registered
\emph{hard} mode: eigenvalues above $2\cdot\mathrm{trace}(S)/B$); (iv)
projects the uniform sample-weight vector onto the kept eigensubspace and
applies the resulting weights to the \emph{unnormalized} gradients to form
the update passed to AdamW. Because anti-aligned (cancelling) components can
be discarded, the filtered update norm is not bounded by the mean-gradient
norm---the filter can amplify as well as shrink, which turns out to matter
(Section~\ref{sec:exp004}).

\subsection{Data and era-purged protocol}
\label{sec:method-data}

We use the public Numerai v5.0 dataset (obfuscated financial features,
weekly eras, $\sim$20-day forward-return target): 705 ``medium'' features,
per-sample MSE on the centered target. To fit the compute profile, training
uses a subsampled shard of 384{,}000 rows (150 eras, 0425--0574,
$\leq$2{,}560 rows/era), identical across all arms. The validation-side eras
are split once, in advance (amendment F1), into a \textbf{tuning block}
(eras 0579--0966; 388 eras), an \textbf{embargo} (0967--0970; 4 eras
$= \lceil 20\,\mathrm{d}/5\,\mathrm{d}\rceil$, amendment F5), and a
\textbf{verdict block} (0971--1225; 255 eras). All tuning, mode selection,
and peeking touch only the tuning block; the verdict block was evaluated
exactly once, after all arms and configurations were frozen (corroborated by
git commit ordering). Sanity and leakage bands were recalibrated from
current example-model statistics (amendment F9): sanity band
$[0.0087, 0.0522]$ mean per-era \texttt{numerai\_corr}, leakage gate
$>0.0696$. The metric is per-era \texttt{numerai\_corr} (with Spearman as a
variant), following practitioner protocol
\citep{numerai_forum2021_eras, numerai_forum2021_fnc}.

\subsection{Pre-registered verdict machinery}
\label{sec:method-verdict}

The deliverable is a three-way verdict with teeth:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Helps / hurts}: the paired per-era CI on the
    filter-on $-$ filter-off difference excludes zero \emph{and} the point
    estimate exceeds a practical-significance threshold $F3$, defined
    as the minimum of $0.005$ and $0.25\times$ the realized tuned-baseline
    mean per-era corr, and frozen before unblinding at $\mathbf{0.00398}$
    (from the 3-seed, winner's-curse-corrected baseline mean $+0.01594$).
  \item \textbf{Doesn't help}: an equivalence-style claim---the CI is
    narrow enough to exclude improvements $\geq F3$---not $p > 0.05$.
  \item \textbf{Inference}: per-era scores are autocorrelated (measured
    lag-1 $\mathrm{acf} = +0.25$; the target horizon spans $\sim$4 eras),
    so all CIs use a paired moving-block bootstrap with block length
    $L = 4$ and 10{,}000 resamples (amendment F5). A corr-Sharpe secondary
    route uses the identical machinery (amendment F10).
  \item \textbf{Tuning-adequacy downgrade (amendment F12)}: tuning budgets
    are matched in \emph{compute}, not trial count. AdamW received a
    12-trial sweep ($\sim$44\,s); the same compute affords $\sim$1 spectral
    trial (63\,s/run), so the spectral arm ran at priors transferred from
    the prior project. This asymmetry was recorded before unblinding as an
    under-exploration signature, with a binding rule: a ``hurts'' verdict
    must be downgraded to \emph{``no evidence of benefit under the
    affordable tuning budget''}, reported with the raw numbers alongside.
    That rule fired, and the downgraded wording is used throughout this
    paper.
  \item \textbf{Engagement diagnostics}: every run logs the number of kept
    eigendirections $k$, kept-energy fraction, update-norm ratio, and
    cosine diagnostics, so a null reads ``mechanism engaged but did not
    help'', not ``implementation no-op''.
\end{itemize}

per unit time from attacking the riskiest cheap components first), executed
as five experiments packed along the dependency structure.

$P_{\text{success}}$ estimates and outcomes.

Before execution, three independent adversarial reviews (assumption
analysis, mentor review, pre-mortem) produced 13 binding protocol
amendments (F1--F13: era separation, frozen thresholds, block-bootstrap
inference, compute-matched tuning with the downgrade rule, conditional cut
order protecting mechanism controls, and claim-naming rules) and 5
diagnostic additions (C1--C5), all folded into the plan before any
comparison was unblinded. The most consequential additions were C2 (a
permuted-target null-spectrum control) and C4 (a random-subspace control
matched to the measured kept-$k$ and update-norm trajectories), which
between them produced both mechanistic contributions.

\subsection{Models, arms, and compute}
\label{sec:method-models}

\textbf{Architectures.} (i) MLP 705--256--64--1 (197k parameters); (ii) a
hand-rolled functional single-layer GRU (hidden 64, 21.8k parameters,
vmap-compatible; sequence-to-one head) consuming each row's 705 features
reshaped to a $T{=}15 \times D{=}47$ within-era sequence. Both use
within-era minibatches of $B{=}1024$ (amendment F6), 2000 steps, base
configuration \texttt{t07} (lr $10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-3}$, dropout 0.2)
selected by the AdamW sweep on tuning-block eras only.

\textbf{Arms} (identical shard, seeds, and data order): \emph{filter-off}
(tuned AdamW); \emph{filter-on} (SpectralConsensusFilter, hard mode,
mp\_factor 2.0---registered from engagement diagnostics only, amendment
F2); \emph{C4 random-subspace control} (random orthonormal sample-subspace
with $k(t)$ and update-norm-ratio$(t)$ matched step-by-step to the
same-seed filter-on run); a \emph{GAF-style sign-agreement ablation}
(per-coordinate $|\sum_i \operatorname{sign}(g_{i})| \geq 2\sqrt{B}$; a
sign-agreement variant, not the exact microbatch-cosine method of
\citet{chaubard2024gradientagreementfiltering}); and a \emph{zero-predictor}
sanity baseline. Three seeds per arm (extended to six on the headline by the
audit re-execution, Section~\ref{sec:robustness}).

\textbf{Compute.} All GPU work ran on single NVIDIA L40s (48\,GB) on a
shared Slurm cluster's free compute partition, every job under 10 minutes;
total producer GPU spend $\sim$15 minutes across four jobs, plus
$\sim$9 minutes for the audit re-execution. Analysis ran on local CPU.
